# Design System (Corporate Light Mode)

## 🎨 Color Palette & Tokens

### Primary System Colors
- **Background (`--bg-color`)**: `#f8fafc` (Off-white / Slate-50)
- **Panel Background (`--panel-bg`)**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)` (Crisp white with backdrop blur)
- **Panel Border (`--panel-border`)**: `rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.9)` (Subtle light slate border)
- **Text Color (`--text-color`)**: `#0f172a` (Deep Slate / Monochromatic Black)
- **Muted Text (`--text-muted`)**: `#64748b` (Corporate Slate Gray)

### Accent & Interaction Colors
- **Accent Color (`--accent-color`)**: `#0f172a` (High-contrast Stark Black)
- **Accent Hover (`--accent-hover`)**: `#1e293b` (Dark Charcoal)
- **Danger Color (`--danger-color`)**: `#ef4444` (Refined Red)
- **Success Color (`--success-color`)**: `#10b981` (Refined Emerald)

### Button Typography & Contrast Standard
- **Primary Buttons**: Black Background (`#0f172a`), Pure White Text (`#ffffff`).
- **Secondary Buttons**: White Background (`#ffffff`), Black Text (`#0f172a`), Slate Border (`#cbd5e1`).
- **Inputs & Selects**: White Background (`#ffffff`), Black Text (`#0f172a`), Slate Border (`#cbd5e1`).

### Map Aesthetics (Light Theme)
- **Unassigned Country Fill**: `#ffffff` (Pure White)
- **Country Boundary Line**: `#cbd5e1` (Light Gray Stroke)
- **Country Hover State**: `#e2e8f0` (Light Slate Hover)
- **Map Ocean / Background**: `#f1f5f9` (Light Slate Background)

---

## 📐 Layout & Typography Rules
- **Font**: `'Outfit', sans-serif`
- **Theme Philosophy**: High-contrast, clean corporate aesthetic, crisp black & white elements, light gray containers, zero unnecessary clutter.
